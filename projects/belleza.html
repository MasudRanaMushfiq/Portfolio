Belleza is a multi-country online beauty and cosmetics marketplace with 80+ brands across skincare, body care, haircare, make-up, fragrance and K-Beauty. I built it end to end: a Laravel RESTful backend with a full admin panel, and a bilingual English / Arabic Next.js storefront on top of it.

Problem

Belleza sells to shoppers in several countries, each with its own currency, and customers pay through the two gateways people in Bahrain actually use. The business also needed a single back office to run the catalog, orders, purchasing, delivery and marketing, instead of spreading that work across separate tools.

Solution

Built a Laravel RESTful API as the single backend for the storefront and the admin panel, with country-aware pricing in local currencies, CrediMax and BENEFIT payment integration, Twilio OTP verification, guest checkout, country- and weight-based shipping charges, gift cards, and automatic back-in-stock emails. The bilingual Next.js storefront consumes the API, and admins and moderators run the whole operation from a role-based admin panel. The backend is modular so new countries, payment methods and features can be added without rewriting the core.

Challenges & Engineering Deep Dive

Many Countries, Many Currencies, One Catalog

Customers from different countries can buy any product, and each country has its own currency, exchange rate and shipping rules. Keeping one catalog while showing every shopper correct local prices and delivery costs was the core design problem.


How I solved it: countries are configured in the admin panel with their own currency, exchange rate and shipping settings. The API resolves the shopper's country and returns prices in that currency, and shipping is charged from that country's rate table according to the order's total weight. The catalog stays single-source while every market sees its own pricing and delivery cost.

Two Payment Gateways, One Checkout

Shoppers in Bahrain expect to pay by card through CrediMax or with BENEFIT. Each gateway has its own flow and callbacks, and orders and gift cards must only be confirmed once payment is verified.


How I solved it: both gateways sit behind a shared payment layer in the Laravel backend, so the checkout and order lifecycle stay the same whichever method the customer picks, and adding another gateway later does not touch the order logic.

Running the Whole Business From One Admin Panel

Beyond products and orders, the team needed purchasing from suppliers, delivery agents and settlements, financial reporting, marketing campaigns and site content, all in one place and without giving everyone full access.


How I solved it: the backend is split into focused modules: delivery, purchasing, catalog, orders, customers, marketing, content and configuration. Admin and moderator roles control who can reach each module, which keeps the system maintainable as features grow.

Bilingual Storefront on a Shared API

The storefront serves English and Arabic, which reads right-to-left, across several countries, while staying fast on an image-heavy catalog.


How I solved it: locale- and country-prefixed routes such as /en/bh drive language and market, the layout mirrors for Arabic from the same components, and product images are served through Next.js image optimization.

Turning Out-of-Stock Into a Sale Later

Popular K-Beauty products often sell out, and a shopper who lands on an unavailable product usually leaves and does not come back.


How I solved it: out-of-stock products show a Notify Me button that records the shopper's request. When the product becomes available again, the backend automatically emails everyone who asked, bringing them straight back to the product.

Low-Friction Checkout That Stays Trustworthy

Forcing every shopper to register loses sales, but orders still need a real, reachable phone number for delivery and to keep fake orders out.


How I solved it: guests can buy without an account, while Twilio sends a one-time code to verify the customer's phone number. Shoppers get a quick checkout and the business gets verified contact details on every order.
